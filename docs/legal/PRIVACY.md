## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how GrokParse (“the Service”, “the tool”) handles information when you use the website, self-hosted build, or open-source software at [github.com/chayprabs/grok-pattern-playground](https://github.com/chayprabs/grok-pattern-playground).

**GrokParse is a browser-only application.** After the initial page load, log samples, grok patterns, match results, and custom pattern definitions are processed on your device. We do not provide a backend that receives, stores, or analyzes your log content.

If you do not agree with this policy, do not use the Service.

---

## 2. Information we do not collect from your use of the tool

We do **not** intentionally collect, receive, or store:

- Log lines, patterns, corpora, or match results you enter in the editor  
- Filenames or file contents you paste or upload into the browser  
- Custom patterns saved in your browser (except on your device — see Section 3)  
- Account credentials for GrokParse (the Service has no login)

We do not sell personal information. We do not use your log content for advertising, model training, or analytics that contain your inputs.

---

## 3. Local storage on your device

Custom grok patterns may be stored in your browser’s **localStorage** (or equivalent) only on your device. You can remove them using the “Clear custom patterns” control in the app or by clearing site data in your browser.

You are responsible for data on devices you control, including backups, shared computers, and browser profiles.
